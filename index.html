Delay periods are split at each rate change

Where a default runs across 25.09.2008 or 14.06.2024, the delay is apportioned. Each part is charged at the rate in force during that part, and the slab for each part is fixed by that part's own length — not by the total delay.

VISHWAS only touches the post-14.06.2024 part

The flat 1% per month is replaced by the graded 0.25 / 0.50 / 1.00%. Everything accruing before 14.06.2024 keeps the annual rate that already applied, so a long-running default may see little or no reduction.
